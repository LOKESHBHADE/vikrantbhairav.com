Jaap Counter

A simple digital mala — tap to count your mantra japa, track rounds of 108, and stay present in your sadhana.

Tap the circle for each repetition of your mantra

How to Use

  • Tap the circle once for every repetition of your mantra
  • The ring fills as you approach one mala — change the mala size above if you prefer 27, 54, or 1008
  • When you complete a mala it's saved automatically and the counter resets for the next round
  • Your progress is saved on this device, so you can close the page and continue later
  • Works with any mantra — the Bhairav mantras are a good place to start
